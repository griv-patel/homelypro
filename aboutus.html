About Us

Empowering Workers.
Serving Communities.

Homely Pro is a cooperative-owned digital platform that connects skilled workers from Labour Cooperative Federations and Societies with households and institutions in need of reliable services.

Our mission is to ensure fair opportunities, dignified work, and stronger communities.

👤

Our Mission

To build a technology-driven cooperative ecosystem that empowers skilled workers, ensures fair wages, promotes social security, and delivers trusted services to every household and community.

👁️

Our Vision

To become the most trusted cooperative service marketplace that strengthens local economies, supports worker welfare, and creates a more inclusive digital future.

⚙️

Why Homely Pro?

  • Owned and managed by Labour Cooperatives
  • Verified and skilled cooperative workers
  • Fair wages, transparency & trust
  • Secure payments and insurance protection
  • Serving households, institutions & communities
  • Building stronger, self-reliant cooperative networks
